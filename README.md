## Favorite Series: *Person of Interest*

**Person of Interest** is a sci-fi crime drama created by Jonathan Nolan about an artificial intelligence known as `The Machine` that predicts acts of terror and violent crimes in New York City.

> "You are being watched. The government has a secret system—a machine that spies on you every hour of every day."

### Main Characters
* **Harold Finch** – A reclusive billionaire software genius who built `The Machine`.
* **John Reese** – A former CIA operative hired to intervene and save people on the list.
* **Sameen Shaw** – A skilled former ISA operative with a blunt, tactical approach.
* **Root (Samantha Groves)** – A brilliant hacker who becomes the analog interface for `The Machine`.

### Top 3 Reasons I Recommend It
1. **Grounded AI Themes:** Explores surveillance, privacy, and artificial superintelligence years before modern AI became mainstream.
2. **Strong Character Arcs:** Every main character evolves significantly across its five seasons.
3. **High-Rated Finale:** Consistently ranks among the highest-rated series finales on TV.
